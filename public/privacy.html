Privacy

Pro Skills Digital Tambayan is a just-for-fun virtual hangout for our team. This page explains what it keeps about you.

What we keep

  • Your account: your email address, and a password if you sign up with one. If you use "Continue with Google", we receive your email address and basic profile from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Your profile: the username, first name, role, batch, chibi look and birthday (month and day, with an optional year) that you choose to enter. Your birth year is not shown to other members.
  • Chat: messages, photos, reactions and pins you send in the public room, batch rooms and private chats, so they are still there when you come back.
  • Activity while you are online: which room your chibi is in and your status. This is shown to other members live and is not kept as a history, apart from the time you were last seen.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your information, and we do not show adverts.
  • We do not use your information for anything other than running this hangout.

Where it is stored

Accounts and chat are stored with Supabase, and the site is hosted on Vercel. Music streams come from SomaFM, and GIFs from Giphy, Tenor or Imgur when you choose to use them; those services can see your device's network address when you load their content.

Your choices

You can change your profile at any time under "My avatar". To have your account and messages removed, contact the team admin at liezljamindang@gmail.com.
